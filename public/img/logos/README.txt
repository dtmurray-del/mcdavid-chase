Notes
- St. Louis also works as nhl-st.-louis-blues-logo.png or nhl-saint-louis-blues-logo.png.
- Utah also works as nhl-utah-hockey-club-logo.png.
- Capitals, spelling and hyphens matter on the live site: use lowercase letters only.
- Any logo that is missing is replaced by the team's three-letter abbreviation, so nothing breaks.
- Add ?debug to the site address during a game to see which logos were found.
